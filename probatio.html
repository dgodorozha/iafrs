PPRBProbatio

An agentic scenario lab for financial stability supervisors

Probatio is a decision first sandbox that stress tests AI market surveillance tools and intervention rules against synthetic AI agent markets, calibrated from an authority’s own data and extended with its own scenarios and agents.

A separate platform

Probatio is separate from the Institute, with its own governance. The Institute’s research informs the platform’s method, and the Institute uses the platform to produce the evidence it publishes; it does not develop, licence or operate it, and it does not benefit from its sale.

The description on this page is of the platform as the Institute uses it. Authorities wishing to run Probatio on their own infrastructure should approach the platform directly; the Institute is glad to make an introduction and to help design a battery.

What it does

It certifies tools on the decision that matters, with a conformal false alert guarantee, a winner stability figure and a walk forward test of the certification itself, and it shows that even the certified tool can be gamed. Every run passes two human gates, whose approver is checked against a register of regulated persons, and 31 critic checks; it leaves a signed, replayable ledger, a transparency record and an evidence pack, and answers the needs that the FSB, IOSCO, the Bank of England and the IMF have flagged.

RRUN

How a run proceeds

Seven stages, two of them gates at which a named human can refuse.

  1. Policy questionRead deterministically, or planned by a model within hard bounds.
  2. PlanThe battery is hashed: demonstration, question, own and calibrated families.
  3. Gate 1The approver, checked against the register, signs off the battery by hash or refuses with a recorded reason.
  4. Battery, rules, assuranceSentinels, rules, guarantee, attribution, audits, red team, backtests.
  5. Critic31 checks recompute every number from the raw rows and replay the ledger.
  6. Gate 2The approver releases the briefing, or refuses.
  7. Briefing and evidence packGraded, vetted and replayable, with the transparency record.
GGOV

The controls

Governed like a supervisory system, not a research artefact.

Human in the loop

Two mandatory gates: the approver signs off the battery by hash, then releases the briefing, or refuses with a recorded reason. The approver must be on the authority’s register of regulated persons (an active senior management or certified function, matched by reference number and written into the ledger), must differ from the preparer, and can stop a run at any moment. Every rule carries its human oversight class; the learned policy is advisory only.

Auditability and traceability

An HMAC signed, hash chained ledger of every step, replayable by a third party; the battery, persona, agent and family definitions hashed and bound at Gate 1; 31 critic checks that recompute every number from the raw rows; an evidence pack with a manifest, a transparency record in the UK algorithmic transparency recording standard structure, and an exchange record.

Safety and governance

No generated figure reaches a supervisor: the critic vets every number in the briefing and the transparency record, and the model, when used, only plans within hard bounds, distils personas and phrases text. Conformal certification bounds false alerts; the autonomy gate defaults to no execution; an injection suite of eleven cases must be contained on every run; roles (viewer, preparer, approver, admin) gate every action.

Cyber risk

A security framework of seventeen controls that block release on a failure: keys from the environment only, security headers, request and rate limits, a SHA-256 manifest over every vendored asset, HTTPS and an egress allowlist for connectors and models, a quarantine for every dataset, classification with no write down, and identity from an authenticating proxy.

Probatio, an agentic scenario lab

A public authority stress tests surveillance sentinels and intervention rules on synthetic AI agent markets calibrated from its own data, and certifies on decision accuracy rather than aggregate fidelity. Every run passes two human gates and a critic, and leaves a signed, replayable ledger.
