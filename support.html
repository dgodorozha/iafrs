Support the Institute

Independent evidence about AI in markets needs independent support. There are three ways to give it.

GGIV

Give

Gifts and grants fund the open benchmark, independent audits of the certification procedure and the researchers’ time. The Institute is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organisation, so gifts from United States taxpayers are deductible to the extent the law allows.

Write to us about a gift
PPRT

Partner

Central banks and market regulators can run a pilot on their own data and their own scenarios, on their own infrastructure. The Institute helps design the battery, reads the evidence with the authority and reports it with its intervals, grades and limitations.

Propose a pilot
CCLB

Collaborate

Researchers in statistics, market microstructure, reinforcement learning and financial regulation are welcome to work with the Institute on the benchmark, on new scenario families and on the certification procedure itself.
