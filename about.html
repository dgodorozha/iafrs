About the Institute

An independent, non-profit research institute for artificial intelligence, financial risk and stability, constituted in the United States and based in Austin, Texas.

Mission

Artificial intelligence is changing who trades, how they react and how quickly a routine shock can become a dislocation. The authorities responsible for financial stability are building surveillance tools and intervention rules for that market, and need a way to know which of them to trust before an episode, not after.

The Institute exists to produce that evidence: to validate tools and rules on the supervisory decisions they inform, with guarantees where guarantees are possible, with every number computed and recomputed, and with a trail a third party can replay. Its work is published for any authority to use.

What we do

Research

Decision first certification, agentic markets and herding, intervention rules, and the governance of generated evidence. The programme is set out under Research.

Evidence for supervisors

Calibrated batteries run with authorities on their own data, read with them, and reported with intervals, evidence grades and limitations.

Open methods

An open benchmark of scenario families and agent templates, so that results can be compared against the same truth and reproduced.

PPRN

Principles

Decide on the decision

A tool is judged by whether it identifies the destabilising agents and the moment to act, not by how well a synthetic market resembles a real one.

Compute, never generate

No figure reaches a reader that was not computed from artefacts and recomputed by an independent check. A language model, when one is used, plans within hard bounds and phrases text; it does not produce numbers.

Guarantee what can be guaranteed

Where a finite sample bound exists, as for the false alert rate under a conformal threshold, it is stated; where it does not, an interval and an evidence grade are.

Keep a human at the gate

Named people, checked against a register and distinct from the preparer, approve a battery and release a briefing, and can refuse or stop at any moment.

Leave a trail

Every step is written to a signed, hash chained ledger that a third party can replay; every battery and every definition it used is hashed and bound before it runs.

Say what it cannot show

Limitations are reported beside results: the calibration shift on an authority’s data, the regret of the certification procedure, the evasions a red team found.

Structure

The Institute is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organisation based in Austin, Texas. It is led by its founder and director, Daria Godorozha, with a research team in statistics, law and machine learning.

Independence

The Institute is separate from Probatio, the platform that implements its method. It does not develop, licence or operate the platform, and it publishes what the evidence shows about any tool or rule, including the certified ones.

Funding

The Institute is funded by gifts and grants. It does not accept funding on terms that would restrict what it publishes, and it names its supporters. See Support.
