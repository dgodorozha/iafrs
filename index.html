A non-profit research institute

Research for a financial system in which the traders are machines.

The Institute studies how artificial intelligence changes financial risk, and how the authorities responsible for stability can validate, on the decisions that matter, the surveillance tools and intervention rules meant to contain it.

Three synthetic markets receive the same shock at step 280: one in which the trading agents are independent, one in which agents on a shared foundation model herd, and one in which a dynamic throttle engages 25 steps later. Drawn from a seeded simulation, not from market data.
WWHT

What we do

Six lines of work, towards a financial system that stays stable when the traders are machines.

TTLS

Build open source tools to measure market risk

Surveillance sentinels, intervention rules and the procedure that certifies them, published for any authority to run on its own data.

DDAT

Work with industrial partners to provide open source data sets

Scenario families and synthetic markets with a known truth, calibrated with partners and released for research and supervision.

LLLM

Contribute to market focused LLM research

How language model agents trade, herd and respond to intervention, and how their behaviour can be supervised.

SSTB

Promote market stability through our research

Evidence for the authorities responsible for stability, reported with intervals, evidence grades and limitations.

EEDU

Provide educational opportunities

For students and practitioners in statistics, machine learning and financial regulation, through the Institute’s projects and teaching.

OOUT

Engage in outreach through events and hackathons

Public events and hackathons that bring researchers, supervisors and industry to the same problems.

Independent evidence needs independent support.

Gifts and grants fund the open benchmark, independent audits of the certification procedure and the researchers’ time. Authorities can partner with the Institute to run a pilot on their own data.
