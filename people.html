People

Statisticians, a lawyer and a machine learning researcher, who have built and audited the method together since the first concept note.

Daria Godorozha

DLEAD
Founder and Director

Daria is completing an MSc in Statistics by Research (Finance) at the London School of Economics, with a dissertation on construct validity in language model evaluation, and holds an LLM in international financial law from King’s College London and an MSc in AI and Data Science.

She co leads research teams at the Oxford Computational Political Science Group and ETH Zurich, carried out a Bank of England research project on whether banks that lobby are riskier, and has taken four hackathon prizes since 2024, including the CIUK Cluster Challenge as LSE team captain.

Prithika Narayanan

PSTAT
Statistics

Prithika is a statistician with an MSc in Statistics from LSE, after a triple major in mathematics, computer science and statistics at Christ University, Bangalore. As a Pathway Developer at Impact Advantage she builds social return on investment models and measurement frameworks.

She won the 2026 LSE Postgraduate Statistics Practitioners’ Challenge with an SROI model of wage equality initiatives and has published on Bayesian optimisation with Springer Nature. With Daria she presented a taxonomy of geometric space for diffusion models at the Oxford Erlangen conference on the Mathematical Foundations of AI.

Sara Gabrielli Salis

SLAW
Law and policy

Sara is completing an LLM in public international law and human rights law at LSE, after an LLB in transnational law at the University of Luxembourg. She has drafted legal analysis and reports on draft laws in three Luxembourg ministries, at the Conseil d’État and in the European Parliament.

She is Luxembourg’s United Nations Youth Delegate, after two years as its Youth Delegate to the EU, and represented the United Kingdom at the UN Commission on the Status of Women in 2026. With Daria she co leads a research project at the Oxford Computational Political Science Group.

Rajib Ahmed

RML
Machine learning

Rajib is a statistician at the Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade, on a Government Statistical Service placement, and a final year medical student at Cardiff University with an MSc in Health Data Science from LSE.

At Oxford’s Radcliffe Department of Medicine he has spent three years building machine learning models for cardiac MRI, and in 2020 he built an agent based model of COVID-19 spread as a Nuffield Future Researcher. He is a Research Associate at the Oxford Computational Political Science Group and completed the Principles of Intelligence Iliad intensive on AI safety in 2026.

IAFRS Institute for AI, Financial Risk and Stability

An independent, non-profit research institute studying how artificial intelligence changes financial risk, and how the authorities responsible for stability can validate the tools and rules meant to contain it.

A 501(c)(3) non-profit organisation. Austin, Texas.
