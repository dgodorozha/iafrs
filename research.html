Research programme

The Institute’s work asks one question in several forms: when the traders are machines, how should a supervisor decide which tools and rules to trust, and how can that decision be certified and audited?

CCRT

How a surveillance tool is certified

Every sentinel is ranked twice. By fidelity: how closely its stress signal tracks the market’s aggregate statistics, the conventional validation yardstick. By decision accuracy: whether the agents it flags are the ones that were in fact destabilising, which only a generated market can reveal. The decision gap (0 for the same order, 1 for the exact reverse) is 0.68 on the demonstration battery; in 90 per cent of 400 resampled batteries the two orders still disagree. The volatility trigger is first by fidelity and last on the decision.

Certification. Tail dependence is the most accurate tool whose false alert probability on a new quiet market is bounded at 0.25 by a conformal threshold set on eight calibration scenarios. It tops the decision ranking in 98 per cent of resamples. The walk forward test, which certifies on earlier folds and scores on the next, shows a mean regret of 0.27, so the procedure, not the tool, is what is certified.

The science behind the metrics. Precision, recall and F1 against the generator’s truth; a 400 scenario bootstrap for intervals, evidence grades, the probability of inversion and winner stability; Kendall’s tau turned into the gap (1 minus tau over 2); and a split conformal threshold on exchangeable quiet scenarios, which gives a finite sample bound on the false alert probability at level alpha.

IINT

How an intervention rule is scored

Each rule is applied to every herding scenario and to the quiet ones. Containment is the share of the dislocation it removes. The hazard ratio, from a Cox model against the untreated market, is the chance of a crash beginning at any moment relative to doing nothing, so 0.27 means the breaker prevents crashes rather than delaying them. The expected shortfall reduction is the cut to the worst tenth of crashes, and false halts are how often a rule stops a calm market.

The appraisal then asks three regulator’s questions, containment, false halts and proportionality: only the market wide breaker passes all three. Hazard ratios on the battery: breaker 0.27, throttle 0.50, LULD band 0.61, kill switch 0.63, ladder 0.72, venue halt 0.80. No rule halted a quiet market.

Rules are drawn from EU, UK and US style instruments, each carrying its human oversight class; the learned policy, a model based reinforcement learning supervisor solved by value iteration under declared halt and throttle costs, is advisory only.

NNET

Where the risk comes from

Concentration. The share of agents on one model vendor is raised step by step at a fixed correlation and the crash measured each time. Below 30 per cent no run dislocates; from 30 per cent every run does, and the curve is convex (curvature 0.63), so each extra percentage point does more damage than the last. The concentration risk that the FSB and Bank of England reports describe gets a number.

Attribution. Shapley values share the crash among agent groups by silencing each combination and re running the same shock; in the strong herd, vendor A carries 0.106 of the drawdown and vendor B 0.013.

Learned herding. 48 reinforcement learning traders, trained separately on quiet markets by double Q learning, SARSA(λ) and advantage actor critic, converge on the same strategy (0.74) without any shared signal: herding is learned, not imposed, and measured by policy convergence and the momentum slope.

KRSK

Can the answer be gamed, and does it transfer?

The red team and the game

A bandit search for the evasion that hurts the certified tool most while the market still dislocates finds one that drives it to an F1 of 0.00, and the same holds for every other single sentinel. Treated as a game, in which the supervisor picks a tool and the adversary picks an evasion, a fixed choice loses outright, but drawing the armed sentinel at random from a mix (endogeneity 41 per cent, lead lag 30 per cent, volatility trigger 29 per cent) guarantees 0.16. Unpredictability is worth measurable detection.

Truth dependence

Regenerating the battery under four different herding mechanisms leaves tail dependence first every time, so the certification is not an artefact of one model of herding. Even so, the certified tool can be gamed, which is why certification is of the procedure, restated on every calibrated battery, and never of one tool for all time.

Off policy audit

From ten logged runs, an estimate of a rule’s value built on the fitted model lands within 0.01 of the simulated truth; the doubly robust estimator is 0.16 off. A live pilot has only the log and could not tell the difference, which is the case for a generated market with a known truth.

DDAT

What an authority’s own data changes

Calibration shift

Refitting the simulator to an authority’s data, its volatility, impact, who trades with whom and the vendor split observed from a vendor column or inferred from flows, and re running gives a rank correlation of 0.47 with the demonstration ranking, and a different winner. A certification must be re run on calibrated markets; the demonstration battery shows the method, not the answer.

Backtest

On a labelled real tape the volatility trigger alerts 18 steps before the marked episode but fires 3.5 times per 100 quiet steps; the absorption ratio finds all four labelled participants, 54 steps late. Timing and workload are measurable on real data; precision is not, because nobody knows who the innocent were.

MMTH

Method

Synthetic markets with a known truth; sentinels that live over the stream and rules that act on it; an evaluation on the decision, and then an audit of the evaluation.

Synthetic markets with a known truth

Noise traders, fundamentalists, vendor clusters, reinforcement learning traders, distilled language model personas, manipulators and the templates an authority defines, under five generators. The truth is a property of how the market was made.

Sentinels live over the stream, rules act on it

Eight sentinels from the herding and risk literature and eight rules by jurisdiction, each reading only the flags as they stood at that step, so that no rule sees the future.

Evaluate on the decision, then audit the evaluation

A decision evaluator, a critic that re verifies every number, a red team, and a walk forward test of the certification procedure itself.

AAUTReinforcement learning and the science behind the metrics

Reinforcement learning traders. 48 agents trained once on quiet markets by double Q learning, SARSA(λ) and advantage actor critic, acting on discretised momentum and volatility; herding is learned, not imposed, and measured by policy convergence and the momentum slope.

Learned supervisor. Model based reinforcement learning: a tabular Markov decision process fitted from trajectories logged under an epsilon soft policy, solved by value iteration under declared halt and throttle costs. Advisory only.

Off policy evaluation. Per decision importance sampling, a clipped doubly robust estimator and the fitted model, each checked against the simulated truth. The red team is a UCB1 bandit, and the game is solved by linear programme.

Decision metrics. Precision, recall and F1 against the generator’s truth; a 400 scenario bootstrap for intervals, evidence grades, the probability of inversion and winner stability; Kendall’s tau turned into the gap (1 minus tau over 2).

Guarantee. A split conformal threshold on exchangeable quiet scenarios gives a finite sample bound on the false alert probability at level alpha.

Attribution and risk. Shapley values over agent groups under common random numbers; Kaplan Meier and ridge Cox hazards for time to dislocation; value at risk, expected shortfall and a generalised Pareto tail; sentinels from LSV imbalance, Hawkes branching, PCA absorption, Louvain communities and tail dependence.

LLIM

Limits and risks

Every figure the Institute publishes carries an interval, an evidence grade and its limitations. These are the ones that matter most.

Simulation to reality

The simulator is calibrated from an authority’s data and hybrid markets are run on the observed path; the calibration shift (0.47, rank one not preserved) and the rankings are reported, never forecasts.

Goodharting and overreliance

Held out scenario families, a truth dependence audit under four generators, walk forward regret of 0.27, and evidence grades and limitations on every figure.

Fabricated figures

No generated figure reaches a supervisor: every number is computed from artefacts and recomputed by a critic; a model, when one is used, plans within hard bounds, and an injection suite of eleven cases must be contained on every run.

Data sensitivity

The demonstration battery is synthetic. Observed data enters only through a quarantine, with size, type, formula and personal identifier checks and classification with no write down; a pilot would use delayed, pseudonymised venue data under the authority’s own statutory powers.

Residual risk

Even the certified tool can be gamed, so certification is of the procedure, restated on every calibrated battery, never of one tool for all time.
